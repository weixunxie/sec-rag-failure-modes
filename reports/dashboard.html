Key Findings

WinnerTask-Aware retrieval scores 45% fully correct, ahead of Vanilla (40%) and Metadata-Filtered (30%). It is the only strategy that retrieves evidence for both sides of every comparison question (0 retrieval errors on 20 comparison questions).
StructuralMetadata filtering fails every comparison question at retrieval (20 of 20): a single ticker/year filter cannot return the second company or year. Vanilla misses one side in 8 of 20. On single-fact and risk-factor questions the three strategies are close.
GenerationOnce retrieval is right, the remaining errors are incomplete answers. 39 of 46 generation-stage errors are partially correct (score 1 of 2), typically missing a detail the reference answer includes, rather than hallucinated.
CitationsCitation fabrication is rare once it is checked deterministically. Only 10 of 120 answers cite a chunk ID that was not retrieved (Vanilla 2, Metadata 7, Task-Aware 1). The first run's 47 came from a judge that never saw the retrieved text.

Error Stage Distribution (out of 40 per strategy)

A retrieval-stage error means the retrieved chunks did not cover the expected company and year (for comparisons: both sides). Metadata-Filtered has 50% retrieval errors, all on comparison questions; Task-Aware has 0%. Task-Aware's higher generation-error share is the flip side: its failures happen after the right evidence was found.

Answer Correctness by Task Type (0–2 scale)

Cross-company questions show the clearest gap: Task-Aware (1.40) vs Metadata-Filtered (1.00). On cross-year questions Vanilla (1.30) edges Task-Aware (1.10). With 10 questions per type, differences of a few tenths are within noise.

Error Type Breakdown by Strategy

WRONG_ENTITY and WRONG_YEAR come from Metadata-Filtered and Vanilla on comparison questions and disappear under Task-Aware. Most remaining errors are labelled REASONING_ERROR, but nearly all of them are partially correct answers that omit a detail, so the label overstates how often the model reasons incorrectly.

Multi-Dimensional Score Profile

% Correct and "No Retrieval Error" are rescaled to 0–2. Task-Aware leads on correctness, faithfulness and retrieval; the gaps on faithfulness and coverage are small.

Correct vs. Error Counts per Strategy

Task-Aware: 18 correct, 22 generation errors, 0 retrieval errors. Metadata-Filtered: 12 correct, 20 retrieval errors. Vanilla: 16 correct. 40 questions per strategy, so a one-question difference moves accuracy by 2.5 points.

Retrieval Strategies
Vanilla40% correct›

Pure cosine similarity over all 2,916 chunks with no metadata filters. Treats every chunk equally regardless of company or year.

Strengths
  • No query parsing needed
  • Can surface both sides of a comparison when they are semantically close
  • Works across all question types
Weaknesses
  • 20% retrieval error rate: fetches chunks from the wrong company or year
  • Missed one side of the comparison in 8 of 20 comparison questions
Metadata Filtered30% correct›

Pre-filters the Qdrant collection to the question's primary ticker and year before similarity search.

Strengths
  • Clean, on-target context for single-entity questions
  • Ties the other strategies on single-fact and risk-factor questions
  • Faster search on large collections
Weaknesses
  • Retrieval failure on 20 of 20 comparison questions: the filter excludes the second company or year
  • Lowest accuracy overall (30%)
Task-Aware45% correct›

Routes queries by question type. Single-entity questions use metadata filtering; cross-year and cross-company questions split the top-k budget equally between both targets.

Strengths
  • Best overall accuracy (45%)
  • 0 retrieval errors on comparison questions
  • Highest correctness on cross-company questions (1.40 vs 1.00 for Metadata-Filtered)
Weaknesses
  • Requires question metadata (task type, companies, years) at query time
  • Highest generation error rate (55%): with the right evidence retrieved, the remaining failures are incomplete synthesis
